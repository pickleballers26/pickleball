    How points work

    Everyone starts at 0 points, and the board starts in your baseline order. Ties are broken by that baseline. n = 7 players, r = your rank, r2 = your partner's rank, o and o2 = opponent ranks. Ranks come from the end of the previous game day.

    • Singles win: r × (n + 1 − o)
    • Doubles win (team total): ((r + r2) / 2) × ((n + 1 − o)(n + 1 − o2) / 2)
    • Singles loss: −(n + 1 − r) × o. This mirrors the win formula, so a better player losing to a worse one loses more.
    • Doubles loss (team total): −(((n + 1 − r) + (n + 1 − r2)) / 2) × (o × o2 / 2)
    • Splitting doubles wins: the lower-ranked teammate takes 50% + 30% × (rank gap / 6) of the win. A 1 and 5 split 70/30. A 2 and 3 split about 55/45.
    • Splitting doubles losses: the higher-ranked teammate takes that larger share of the loss. If a top-2 player is paired with a 6th or 7th ranked player, their share is capped at 65%, so they still lose a lot but less than with a stronger partner.
    • Daily total: add up a player's match points for the day, divide by the games they played that day (counted automatically as you add games), then multiply by 0.1. The 0.1 keeps every ratio between players intact while limiting how far the board can move in one day.
    • Weekdays only: games are tracked Monday to Friday. Weekend dates can't be logged.
    • Missed days: a game day is a weekday that was closed with at least one game. A player who sits out 3 game days in a row starts to decay on the 3rd, losing 5% of their positive points for each missed game day from then on. Negative points don't decay. The moment they play again the decay is removed and their full points come back.
    • Settling: points count only after you close the day on the Game Logs tab, so the next day plays at the previous day's rankings.
    • Every win earns something and every loss costs something, including a win over the last-ranked player and a loss to the first-ranked player.
